# Impeccable Style System Guidelines (impeccable.style)

## The 4 Modes & Design Vocabulary
1. **Persuade**: Landing pages, heroes, clear distinct identity. High visual craft, purposeful typography, committed colors.
2. **Operate**: App UI, gameplay tools, dashboards. Uncompromising scanability, fast interactions, clear focus.
3. **Read**: Docs, rules, encyclopedias, text. Clean reading rhythm, optical measure, no clutter.
4. **Experience**: Games, dynamic canvas, audio/visual media. Clean layout that puts the game front and center.

## Anti-Slop & Quality Rules (Never violate):
- **Icons**: Always use precision vector SVGs (Lucide / Tabler standards) on 24x24 grid with 2px stroke width. Never sketch amateurish hand-coded paths.
- **Borders & Elevation**: Never use thick colored side-tab borders on rounded cards. Never mix hairline borders with wide fuzzy shadows. Commit to clean edges or soft elevation.
- **Card Nesting**: Avoid "Cardocalypse" (cards inside cards inside cards). Use spacing, dividers, and subtle surface depth instead.
- **Shape & Radii**: Cards are 12-20px radius. Never over-round small cards into 44px blobs. Pill shapes are strictly for buttons, search bars, and tags.
- **No Background Noise**: No random grid-line backgrounds or repeating diagonal stripes.
- **Color & Palette**: Strict semantic tokens. Avoid "purple gradient on everything". High contrast (WCAG AA) for all text.
- **Typography Scale**: Clear hierarchical contrast (at least 1.25x ratio between steps). No text under 11px. No unnecessary tracked uppercase kickers above every single header.
- **Copy & Writing**: Say it once, say it well. Concise, human, action-driven verbs on buttons.
